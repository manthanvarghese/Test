# deep-research checklist

Judgment checks only; structure, citations and placeholders are enforced by verify.sh.

- [ ] The report answers the topic as scoped, not an adjacent one.
- [ ] At least 3 genuinely different perspectives were covered, not one view restated.
- [ ] Every claim in Findings comes from a source that was actually read.
- [ ] Disagreements between sources are shown, not smoothed over.
- [ ] Open questions names every platform or source that could not be reached.
- [ ] Summary matches Findings (no claim appears only in the summary).
