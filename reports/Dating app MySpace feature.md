# Building Dating's MySpace Without Spotify's Keys

**Build a constrained, scene-based "Room" that users furnish with their taste, and feed it from embeds, user-supplied exports and light verification rather than from deep platform APIs.** The data dream is the weakest leg of the idea in 2026. Spotify Development Mode now allows only 5 authorized users and requires a Premium-holding owner, and Extended access is reserved for established, scalable apps ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/06/spotify-changes-developer-mode-api-to-require-premium-accounts-limits-test-users/)). LinkedIn gives third parties sign-in and a verification badge but no rich profile import. Strava, Instagram, Goodreads and Letterboxd are closed or hostile. The space itself is the more achievable half: a closed catalogue of platform-authored objects, rendered from a structured spec on 2D tooling, fits low-end Android and keeps moderation tractable. Prior art points the same way. MySpace-style raw HTML freedom produced worms, phishing and clutter, while template-plus-constrained-freedom products (Canva, Discord cosmetics, Snap's Bitmoji) kept users creating. Our recommended concept is a data-furnished isometric room, launched as a fixed-slot "Room-lite" (about 4-6 months, roughly 3-5 engineers plus 2 artists) before opening to free placement. Monetization should come from cosmetic and subscription layers, not from selling reach. The largest risks are legal: inferred sensitive traits, platform terms, minors and India's 2-3 hour takedown clocks. **Caveat on evidence:** the six research notes behind this report are partial. Most sources were search summaries or secondary and vendor blogs, several primary sources (Spotify, LinkedIn, Figma) were blocked, and all effort estimates and concept designs are my inference, not findings.

## What this report can and cannot claim

Treat the report as a decision memo built on uneven evidence. The strongest material concerns platform access (dated announcements from TechCrunch, Spotify and Apple documentation), recent incidents (Tea, Panama Playlists, Roblox litigation) and public earnings. The weakest material concerns what actually makes people spend hours decorating a space inside a dating app. No source studied that, and none measured whether customization improves dating outcomes. Spotify's primary developer pages could not be fetched, so claims about quotas, caching and branding rest on news coverage and forum replies. The roughly 250,000 monthly-active-user bar for Spotify Extended access appears only in community posts, not in official documents ([Spotify community](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Spotify-for-Developers/Web-API-Limitations/td-p/7511737)). Likewise, the Feb 2026 migration date for existing dev-mode apps is contested. Whenever a claim below is my reasoning rather than a sourced fact, it is labelled "inference" or "estimate". Anything touching law is research context, not legal advice.

## Spotify is display-only, LinkedIn is a badge, and the rest is embeds and exports

The realistic integration set in 2026 is smaller than the brief imagines, and it sorts into three tiers.

**Spotify.** Since 11 February 2026, new Development Mode apps require a Premium account, one Client ID, at most 5 authorized users (down from 25) and a reduced endpoint set. Existing apps were reportedly migrated on 9 March, and Spotify says dev mode should not be a foundation for a business ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/06/spotify-changes-developer-mode-api-to-require-premium-accounts-limits-test-users/), [Techzine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/devops/138608/spotify-puts-the-brakes-on-developer-mode-with-stricter-api-rules)). Extended access is limited to "established, scalable, and impactful" use cases, and Spotify said over 95% of applications failed basic security, privacy and licensing standards ([Spotify blog](https://developer.spotify.com/blog/2025-04-15-updating-the-criteria-for-web-api-extended-access)). Recommendation, audio-feature and related-artist endpoints were already cut for new apps in November 2024 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/27/spotify-cuts-developer-access-to-several-of-its-recommendation-features)). The developer policy also bars using Spotify content to train ML models, requires deletion when a user disconnects, and restricts how cover art is shown: it must link back, may not be modified or cropped, and cannot be a standalone product ([Spotify Developer Policy](https://developer.spotify.com/policy/), [compliance tips](https://developer.spotify.com/compliance-tips)). It is unverified whether top-tracks and recently-played endpoints survive in dev mode. Spotify is therefore a display-only source at launch: iFrame embeds and user-pasted share links, plus the user's own data export. Whether the embed terms permit this at scale, and whether the "Extended streaming history" export exists in a usable form, are my assumptions to verify. Do not feed Spotify data into matching or recommendation models. A forum reply also suggests combining Spotify data with other services' data likely conflicts with the terms, though this is not an official ruling ([Spotify community](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Spotify-for-Developers/Concerns-About-Implementing-Chartmetric-API-on-a-Spotify/td-p/7322158)).

**LinkedIn.** Self-serve access is Sign In with LinkedIn (OpenID Connect with `openid`, `profile`, `email`), returning a lite profile of ID, name and photo ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-gb/linkedin/consumer/integrations/self-serve/sign-in-with-linkedin-v2)). Whether it includes headline is unconfirmed. The more interesting option is **Verified on LinkedIn**, a 2025 self-serve API whose free Lite tier (5,000 requests per day) lets third-party apps display a member's verified identity and workplace flags with consent ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/consumer/integrations/verified-on-linkedin/overview)). LinkedIn says it is for trust enhancement, not eligibility decisions, and requires unaltered branding ([LinkedIn news](https://news.linkedin.com/2025/Verified_on_LinkedIn)). Rich import of positions and education is not part of that product, and LinkedIn is suing scrapers: Proxycurl shut down in July 2025 after litigation and ProAPIs is being sued ([LinkedIn news](https://news.linkedin.com/2025/linkedin-takes-legal-action-to-defend-member-privacy), [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/artificial-intelligence/linkedins-war-against-bot-scrapers-ramps-up-as-ai-gets-smarter)). Archived API terms bar storing returned data and showing one member's network data to other users, and LinkedIn's rules describe it as a professional network, not a place for romance ([LinkedIn API terms](https://developer.linkedin.com/legal/api-terms-of-use)). The current terms text and whether Verified on LinkedIn tolerates dating use were not confirmed. Our plan is to treat LinkedIn as an optional verified-badge source after a legal read, with employer and role supplied by the user or confirmed through a work-email OTP (inference). Daters do value LinkedIn credibility: a vendor survey says 48% trust LinkedIn more than dating-app profiles ([Zety](https://zety.com/blog/linkedin-romance-report)), but that is a résumé company's directional data. The League and The Inner Circle use LinkedIn for admission screening rather than for in-profile import ([Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/the-league-tests-linkedin-for-dating-app-marketing/)).

One consequence is strategic. If the space cannot pull deep data automatically, **the act of choosing becomes the content**. Picking five albums for your shelf, pasting a trail you ran, or uploading a Letterboxd export is itself self-expression, and it keeps users in control of what is shown. This matches the safety logic below, where abstract, user-chosen artefacts beat raw feeds.

## Avatar and space generation: own the asset format, start 2D

Ready Player Me is a cautionary tale for any plan built on a third-party 3D avatar vendor. Netflix announced its acquisition in December 2025, and services were scheduled to shut on 31 January 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/19/netflix-acquires-gaming-avatar-maker-ready-player-me/), [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/netflix-acquires-xr-avatar-startup-ready-player-me/)). Confirmation that the shutdown completed comes mainly from competitor vendor blogs, and no Netflix avatar launch was found. Zepeto's 2025 revenue fell 14.4% to 66.3 billion won despite a claimed 400 million cumulative users ([Newsis](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260410_0003586640)). Snap's forced switch to 3D Bitmoji drew a petition of nearly 100,000 signatures and a 2D "Comic Bitmoji" return for Snapchat+ subscribers in November 2025 ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/11/11/snapchat-reintroduces-2d-avatars-after-users-rejected-3d-ones/)). The lessons, as inference: own the asset format (layered 2D or glTF/VRM parts), offer a stylised or abstract default, and never force a redesign. No major dating app was found shipping avatar profiles in 2026. Big-app moves were AI matching, camera-roll "vibe" insights and verification ([Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/tinders-new-ai-features-scan-camera-rolls-for-vibe-insights/)), so this space is open but unproven.

**The options, and my effort estimates.** No source supplied build costs. The tiers below are inference to be validated with engineering, and the art content cadence, not code, is likely the recurring cost.

| Option | What it is | Rough effort (estimate) | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Template and widget spaces | Themed skins, stat cards, an LLM-written caption, a parametric SVG/Rive avatar | 2-4 engineers plus 1 designer, 2-4 months | Strong; cheap, controllable |
| B. 2D room or island with closed catalogue | Drag-and-drop objects, server sync, shareable render | 4-7 people (mobile, backend, 2 artists, designer, QA), 6-9 months, plus ongoing art | Strong if scoped via slots first |
| C. Generative scene images | Few AI-generated "vibe scenes" per user | Low engineering; moderation and retries dominate | Premium or one-off moments |
| D. Selfie-to-3D avatar (MetaPerson, Avaturn) | Vendor SDK, biometric data | Medium, vendor-risk and face-data obligations | Avoid at launch |
| E. Full 3D world, Unity-as-library | Visitable spaces, multiplayer | 10-20 people, 12-24 months, heavy safety ops | Avoid |

On cost, image generation is cheap per unit: roughly $0.014-0.04 per image from major vendors in August 2026 ([Invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/ai-image-model-pricing/)), so five generations per signup costs about $0.05-0.20 (my arithmetic). Model churn is the hidden cost, since Imagen 4 Fast and older gpt-image versions were tied to 2026 shutdown dates ([Invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/ai-image-model-pricing/)). Aggregators disagree on figures, so verify on vendor pages. A cheaper and more controllable path is procedural rendering from structured data, with generation reserved for premium moments (inference).

**Engineering fit.** Rive runtimes are open source (editor seats are the cost, with conflicting plan figures across aggregators) ([Rive docs](https://rive.app/docs/runtimes/getting-started.md)). Unity-as-a-library works through community bridges, but sources cite roughly 300 MB memory in one issue and about 14 MB minimum size overhead in one blog, and no low-end Android benchmarks exist ([GitHub issue](https://github.com/fusetools/react-native-unity2/issues/27)). India's budget tier is tightening: sub-$100 phones fell 74.3% year-on-year in Q2 2026 and memory is now about 43-45% of entry-level bill of materials ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/india-smartphone-market-hits-five-year-low-phones-under-rs-10000-are-fast-disappearing-548685-2026-08-12)), with a single leaker forecasting 4GB-RAM entry phones in 2027 ([Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/android-budget-phones-4gb-ram-2027-3725430)). My inference is a Skia-style canvas plus Rive state machines with sprite atlases, remote-configurable assets, and server-rendered static images of each space for swipe and share surfaces. That stays inside 3-4GB devices more plausibly than Unity, though none of it was benchmarked.

## Customization works when the mechanism is constrained and the start is never blank

**MySpace's real lesson.** danah boyd said in 2006 that most time on MySpace went to modifying one's own profile, uploading photos, messaging and viewing friends' profiles ([Honolulu Advertiser](https://archives-new.honoluluadvertiser.com/article/2006/Jun/11/il/FP606110324.html)). Choosing a Top 8 was an "identity performance" ([JCMC](https://academic.oup.com/jcmc/article/13/1/252/4583067)), and also a drama engine ([LJWorld reprint](https://www2.ljworld.com/news/2006/may/21/myspace_revives_juniorhigh_style_drama/)). Open HTML enabled the Samy worm and phishing profiles ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism_of_Myspace)), and low-authority analyses blame heavy images, autoplay music and widgets for slowness. Loss to Facebook had other causes too (platform strategy, real-identity policy). My reading is that expression was never the problem. Uncontrolled mechanism was. For a dating app, ranked or exclusion-signalling elements deserve extra suspicion given the Top 8 drama.

**Design principles that follow** (principles are my synthesis; supporting evidence is practitioner and gaming research, not dating studies):

1. **Never open on a blank canvas.** Canva's onboarding is described as built around "no blank canvas" ([Supademo](https://supademo.com/content/plg-onboarding-gallery/canva)), and Hinge says 63% of daters struggle to know what to include in a profile ([Hinge](https://hinge.co/press/profile-refresh-guide)). Seed the first space from existing photos and prompts so users have something to react to.
2. **Constrain the mechanism, free the expression.** Render from a structured JSON spec of blocks, theme tokens and curated stickers. No raw HTML, CSS or third-party scripts. Use grid snapping and palette tokens with contrast checks so output stays good-looking. My own comparison ranks the "constrained freeform middle" (block layout with themed skins and curated stickers) best for a mobile dating app; this ranking is inference.
3. **Big choices first, fine control later.** Vibe, palette and layout in the first minute, with "edit more" behind it.
4. **Play, then publish.** A private draft with preview and undo before anything is visible to others (my recommendation; no source studied it).
5. **Make effort visible as ownership.** The IKEA effect (practitioner sources) and avatar-identification studies (Turkay and Kinzer 2014; Kocur et al. 2025) support the claim that customizing builds attachment ([Kocur et al.](https://epub.uni-regensburg.de/78549/1/3706598.3713203.pdf), [DiGRA](https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/download/2809/2793/2852)).
6. **Add context to verified photos, never replace them.** Avatar research shows people pick actual-self, ideal-self or playful versions ([Tampere](https://webpages.tuni.fi/gamification/2025/04/24/avatar-customisation-and-empowerment-in-vr-collaboration-a-new-study-on-self-representation-and-self-perception/)), so an authenticity layer is needed in dating.
7. **Keep AI to layout and styling, not the user's words.** A Bloomberg survey found Gen Z largely unimpressed by AI profile features ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/07/17/dating-apps-ai-gen-z-bloomberg-study/)).

**What the revival market says.** There is demand for self-built, nostalgic pages but it is small and fragile. SpaceHey passed 200,000 signups in 2021 and was later reported at about 1 million users, moderated by a founder and about five volunteers ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2021-11-12/retro-myspace-clone-spacehey-blows-up-with-200-000-signups), [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91184623/spacehey-myspace-clone)). Noplace surged in 2024 and skeptics say it faded ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2024/07/05/noplace-the-myspace-for-gen-z-is-soaring-in-popularity/)). Bento was shut down on 13 February 2026 after Linktree acquired it, and Koji went the same way in 2024 ([AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/12/bento-to-shut-down-in-2026-as-linktree-takes-over-and-offers-migration-path)). Standalone block-profile products struggle commercially, which supports embedding the feature in an app with an existing graph and revenue model (inference). Roblox shows the safety cost of open user-created content: nearly 80 lawsuits were consolidated into an MDL in December 2025, and Roblox denies the allegations ([Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/roblox-facing-mounting-lawsuits-parents-090000161.html)). A closed catalogue avoids that class of problem.

## The dating landscape rewards identity-rich products, but nothing proves a space lifts outcomes

Match Group's Q3 2026 payers fell 5% to 13.8M, with Tinder payers down 4% to about 8.88M while Hinge revenue rose about 19% and payers 23% ([Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/match-group-q3-2026-results-hinge-growth-and-new-plans-for-tinder/)). Bumble revenue fell 15.9% and paying users 15.1% in the same quarter ([Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/bumble-inc-bmbl-q3-2026_3eHBGTFd)), while Grindr grew revenue 22% ([Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/grindr-q3-2026-results/)). Sources disagree on Match's adjusted EBITDA direction. Gen Z swipe fatigue is pushing incumbents toward in-person events, with Tinder launching Events in March 2026 ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/20/dating-apps-tinder-bumble-breeze-irl-events-gen-z-swipe-fatigue/)). The reading, as inference: users reward intent and identity over swipe volume, which fits a creation-first space, but this is an association in company data, not evidence for our feature.

On expressiveness, the only quantified result is Hinge Voice Prompts: the company claims 10% more likes, 2x matches and 1.4x dates, and users already improvise by adding song clips ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/10/27/hinge-launches-a-new-voice-prompts-feature-to-give-users-a-new-way-to-interact/)). Those are company-reported lifts for adding one field, not for a full space. On compatibility, couples show more similarity in music preference than in personality or values, but the link to relationship quality is small ([PsyPost](https://www.psypost.org/musical-compatibility-often-outshines-personality-matching-in-romantic-couples/)). Kudeki and Karahalios found similar listening motivations and perceived individuality mattered more than genre overlap ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-642-40477-1_46)). So surface specific shared items and their stories ("this album on your shelf, and why") as conversation hooks, not a percentage-match score. Whether space creators date better is unknown, and the notes found no cold-start playbooks for creation-first apps adding dating.

## Five concepts for what the space looks like

These designs are my inference, built from the integration, avatar and prior-art findings. They are meant to make the product pictureable, not to claim any has been tested. To keep them concrete, each is shown through one example user, **Meera, 27, a product designer in Bengaluru** who loves indie folk, reads fantasy, climbs on weekends and recently started a plant collection.

**Concept 1: The Room (data-furnished isometric room).** A cutaway room in a warm illustrated style. Opening the app, Meera lands on her room, not a swipe stack. Her five chosen albums sit as sleeves on a record shelf, and tapping one plays a 30-second embed. Her book picks line a shelf with spine colours drawn from the covers, a climbing rope hangs by the window, and a window view shows her city skyline with time-of-day lighting. Plants grow from "interests" she tags. A corkboard holds three photos and her prompts, and a small frame on the desk carries her verified badges (selfie, optional LinkedIn). A "visit" screen shows another user's room, with objects you share glowing softly, so overlaps surface without a score. Edit mode swaps into a catalogue drawer: choose a theme (Midnight Study, Monsoon Balcony, Studio Loft), then swap objects. Effort tier B, and the strongest fit for the "forget it's dating" goal because it resembles a nesting game.

**Concept 2: The Zine (collage scrapbook page).** A tactile page of polaroids, ticket stubs, a hand-torn receipt of top artists, sticker clusters, washi tape and a handwriting font. Meera drags a "receipt" block (her chosen albums styled like a Receiptify slip), a "reading stack" block and a map-pin sticker for her climbing gym. Pages scroll like a magazine spread, and visitors flip through pages. This is the closest descendant of MySpace and the cheapest to build on a block grid (effort A to low B), but it sits nearest to existing profile patterns and rewards design confidence over play. Expressive users will love it. Shy users will freeze unless starter kits are strong.

**Concept 3: The Vibe Creature (abstract avatar and mood-scape).** Meera has a small abstract creature, a soft blob with ears, a tail and a glow, whose shape, colour and idle animation derive from her answers and chosen music moods (calm and acoustic gives a slow, round, warm-toned creature). It lives on a mood-scape that shifts with her "current vibe" status. The creature greets visitors, reacts when you share a taste, and is the core of a profile card in the swipe feed. It sidesteps catfishing and uncanny-valley risk (inference), and rides on a Rive rig, so effort sits at A. Alone it is thin as a "space", so it works best as the character inside another concept.

**Concept 4: The Street (neighbourhood of houses).** Each user has a small house on a shared street, grouped by city or interest, and doors unlock after a match. Matches drop by, leave notes in a mailbox, and see seasonal decorations. This has the strongest "world" feel and social-loop potential, but it is tier E: a visitable multi-user world with real-time presence, heavy safety operations and the highest risk on low-end Android. Defer it.

**Concept 5: The Night Sky (constellation of taste).** Meera's interests become stars, her chosen albums, books and places clustering into constellations she names ("Rainy-Day Folk"). Visiting another user overlays their sky, and shared stars draw bright connecting lines, a literal picture of overlap without a compatibility percentage. Procedurally generated, cheap (tier A) and beautiful, and it conveys compatibility elegantly. It is less nesting-like, so people may admire it rather than tend it.

**Recommendation: Concept 1 (The Room), with Concept 3's creature as an optional resident and Concept 5's shared-star glow as the overlap mechanic.** The Room best serves the "forget it's a dating app" goal because decorating and tending a place is intrinsically absorbing, and the object metaphor maps every constrained data source into a visible, user-chosen item (shelf of albums, stack of books, framed badge) without raw feeds or handles. It also complies with the sourced design principles: closed catalogue, structured spec, template start. The main risk is art production, which is the recurring cost in this class of product (inference). The Zine is the cheapest fallback if art capacity falls short.

### MVP scope

The MVP is **"Room-lite", a fixed-slot room**: roughly 12 slots (shelf, books, window, desk, wall board, plant, rug, lamp and so on), 3 themes and about 60-80 catalogue objects. Each slot is filled from a source the user chose, so the room is generated from structured data by default and editable by swapping objects. Onboarding assembles a starter room from the user's existing prompts and photos, plus a 60-second "furnish your shelf" flow. Included sources: Spotify and YouTube embeds, manual album/book/film pickers with TMDB metadata, CSV imports for Letterboxd and Goodreads, selfie verification, optional Verified on LinkedIn. Surfaces: the room, a visit view with soft overlap glow, and a room thumbnail on the swipe card, rendered server-side. Visibility is private by default, with per-object audience control (public, matches only, chosen). Excluded at MVP: free placement, guestbooks, avatars, AI image generation, Apple Music, Last.fm, gifts and 3D. A team of 3-5 engineers, 2 artists and 1 designer for about 4-6 months is my estimate, unvalidated.

## Engagement and monetization: tend-and-visit loops, cosmetic layers, and a dating-specific tension

**Loops.** Evidence is thin. The best-supported ideas are friend-visible changes, since Snap notifies friends when someone updates their Bitmoji outfit ([Snap newsroom](https://newsroom.snap.com/sps-2023-new-snapchat-features-to-fuel-your-friendships?lang=en-US)), earned cosmetics (Discord Orbs from Quests, per [Discord](https://discord.com/press-releases/discord-launches-orbs-globally)), and no-feed, no-likes visiting as in SpaceHey and Are.na. No A/B retention data was found for guestbooks, remix or seasonal drops. Roblox's Q3 2026 numbers (DAU down 9% year-on-year, hours down about 15%) show how volatile UGC engagement is without recurring content ([gaming.net](https://gaming.net/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion)). Candidate loops, all untested: a "room updated" nudge to matches; seasonal drops of themes and objects; a match-only guestbook; monthly "rearrange" prompts tied to new music or reads; and a weekly curated spotlight of tasteful rooms. Avoid ranked displays (Top 8 lesson) and public likes. Measure by cohort: do creators hold more conversations and convert more dates than non-creators? The notes found no benchmark, so build this experiment in from Phase 1.

**Monetization.** Cosmetic and subscription layers are proven in social apps. Snapchat+ reached 25M subscribers in February 2026 at $3.99 per month ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/18/snapchat-tops-25m-subscribers-driving-companys-direct-revenue-arr-to-1b/)), and Snap cited 30M+ paid subscribers across bundled products by Q3 2026 ([Snap newsroom](https://newsroom.snap.com/snapchat-subscription-offerings-surpass-30-million)). Discord sells avatar decorations and profile effects, with animated effects launched around $5 ([Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/discord-fall-2023-updates-monetization)), and Telegram reports about $44.8M of gift sales in 30 days, from crypto-press sourcing and indicative only ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/telegram-gifts-44-8-million-sales-mrkt-portals/)). Dating apps earn much more per payer (Tinder $18.02, Hinge $31.81 per month) but their payer bases are shrinking ([Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/match-group-q3-2026-results-hinge-growth-and-new-plans-for-tinder/)). My hypothesis: a low-priced cosmetic subscription (themes, rare objects, animations, extra rooms or slots) complements, rather than replaces, premium match features, and suits price-sensitive Indian users. India prices are unreliable in the notes; only Hinge+ at INR 1,199 per month has a reasonable source ([Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/from-the-web/hinge-india-second-largest-market/)). Two cautions. Match shifted payers to alternate payment methods and cut app-store fees by $43M, which helps cosmetic unit economics if app-store rules allow it in our markets (verify). And selling visibility or attention in a dating context creates a pay-to-win concern: cosmetics should change how a room looks, never how often it is shown (design judgement). A cosmetic economy may also skew toward heavy spenders (inference).

## Legal and safety risk concentrates in four places

*Note on sourcing: this is research context, not legal advice, and several primary texts were unread.*

**1. Sensitive inference and platform terms.** In the EU, processing that lets special-category data be revealed by inference falls under GDPR Article 9 (CJEU C-252/21 per [Clifford Chance](https://www.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2023/07/european-court-of-justice-in-facebook-ruling-clarifies-interplay-between-eu-competition-law-and-data-protections-enforcement.pdf), paragraph cites unverified). C-446/21 read "manifestly made public" narrowly ([Thomas Helbing](https://www.thomashelbing.com/en/wissen/dsgvo-hub/rechtsprechung/1.4.30-eugh-schrems-meta)). Norway's Grindr fine of NOK 65m was upheld on appeal in October 2025, with the court treating app-use information as special-category ([Forbrukerradet](https://www.forbrukerradet.no/news-in-english/grindr-loses-appeal/)). Do not infer or display orientation, religion or politics from taste, and take granular per-source consent. Separately, building features that train on Spotify content or surface LinkedIn data to other users likely breaches those platforms' terms. The consequence is feature-ending key revocation, and no enforcement cases against dating apps were found.

**2. Doxxing, stalking and breach.** Spotify's public-by-default settings enabled the 2025 "Panama Playlists" compilation of politicians' and executives' listening ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/spotify-panama-playlists-3582830/)). Strava's 2018 heatmap exposed military patterns ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2018/01/29/581597949/pentagon-reviews-gps-data-after-soldiers-strava-tracks-are-seemingly-exposed)). The July 2025 Tea breach exposed about 72,000 images, including roughly 13,000 selfies and ID photos, from legacy storage ([ABC7](https://abc7.com/post/tea-app-was-intended-help-women-date-safely-got-hacked/17332900/)). Design response (my recommendation): private by default, per-audience visibility, a reveal-after-match layer, abstract artefacts instead of raw handles, playlists, URLs or "now playing", no exact location, store only the verification result and delete selfie or ID material after use, and a match-only guestbook to avoid an unsolicited-contact channel. Evidence that reveal-after-match or verification gating actually reduces harm was not found.

**3. Minors and content.** UK children's codes took effect on 25 July 2025, and Ofcom's 2026 report found over 1 in 10 15-17-year-olds still visiting three leading dating apps ([Ofcom](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/online-safety/information-for-industry/age-assurance-report-2026/report-on-the-use-of-age-assurance.pdf)). The EU's DSA Article 28 guidelines judge self-declaration insufficient ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-guidelines-protection-minors)). Apple and Google require UGC filtering, reporting and blocking, and Apple requires in-app account deletion ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app), [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937)). A closed catalogue plus a ban on sexual content reduces exposure to the stricter "highly effective age assurance" tier (inference), though user-uploaded photos and free text remain the risk.

**4. India.** The DPDP Rules were notified in mid-November 2025. Notice, security, retention and children's provisions commence about 18 months later, around mid-May 2027, with consent-manager rules at about 12 months ([Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/firms/237811-saga-legal/c-india/news-and-developments/the-privacy-timer-starts-now-analysing-the-dpdp-rules-and-its-18-month-countdown)). Section 9 requires verifiable parental consent for children's data and bars tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children ([Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/child-s-personal-data-and-privacy-analysing-the-draft-dpdp-rules-2025)). Some commentary referenced draft rule numbering, and the final text was not read. DPDP has no special-category tier, but the design window is now, and per-source consent and age gating should be architected in before launch. The IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Amendment Rules 2026, in force 20 February 2026, define synthetically generated information, require labelling, and cut takedown time after a valid order from 36 to 3 hours, with one report citing 2 hours for non-consensual intimate content ([Nishith Desai](https://www.nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/technology-law-analysis/deepfakes-disclosure-and-due-diligence-unpacking-indias-sgi-amendments-15532), [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/governance/36-hr-takedown-window-cut-to-3-hrs-centre-notifies-it-rules-amendment-to-tighten-noose-on-deepfakes/2851104)). Missing deadlines risks Section 79 safe harbour ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/telecoms-mobile-cable-communications/1698534/government-sahyog-in-tightening-takedowns)). Operational implication (inference): a 24x7 on-call takedown process, a published grievance officer, AI-content labels, and pre-publish screening of images and video. Aadhaar-based ID checks carry a legal and privacy overhang, so if an ID badge is added it should be a boolean via DigiLocker, never stored identifiers. Whether the amended rules apply to a dating app of our size was not assessed, and no India-specific dating regulation was found. Copyright in music and album art was not researched beyond Spotify's art rules, and trademark exposure for brand assets in generated scenes needs legal review.

## Conclusion

The brief starts from data richness, but the evidence says to start from the object metaphor. With the biggest feeds largely closed, a space that users furnish by choice, from embeds, exports and pickers, is both the only feasible build and the safer one. It also happens to match what the prior art rewards: expression with constrained mechanism, a non-blank start and private play before publish. The unexpected consequence is that the scarcity of data makes the user's curation the product, which strengthens ownership and reduces re-identification risk at the same time.

What remains unproven is the claim that matters most: that a space pulls people into the app and improves dating outcomes. Nothing in the notes tests this, and the one dating-relevant quantified lift (Hinge Voice Prompts) is company-reported and narrow. So the right first commitment is a four-to-six month Room-lite with the cohort experiment built in, plus the term-reading and DPIA work that gate it, rather than a 12-month 3D world. If creators do not converse and date more, the cost of finding out will be small. If they do, Phase 2 earns its art budget.
