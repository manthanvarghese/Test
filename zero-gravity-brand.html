Brand directions

Zero Gravity should feel like exhaling.

Lonely people feel pulled down and pulled everywhere. The brand is the release from that: light, calm, unhurried, and warm enough to trust. Below are three logo directions, three palettes you can try on a screen, and the rules that keep it consistent.

Logo

Three directions

Recommended

1. The floating dot

zero gravity
  • One shape, readable at 40px.
  • A dot hovering over its own shadow says "no pull" without space clichés.
  • The gradient doubles as the dusk signature.

2. Orbit and meet

zero gravity
  • Two people, a shared middle: companionship and "marriage later".
  • Closest to a dating-app idea, so least distinctive.
  • Looks like a Venn diagram if the overlap is too large.

3. The open zero

zero gravity
  • The name becomes the symbol; the gap means "room to breathe".
  • A small warm dot floats inside, which is you, unpressured.
  • Test that it doesn't read as "empty" or "none".

Guardrails

What to avoid and what to check

Motion is the brand

Let avatars and cards drift slowly. No hard snaps or bouncing alerts.

Voice matches visuals

"No rush. No pull." Calm, warm, unhurried copy. No pushy notifications.

Accessibility

Use the indigo or ink for text. Light lavender text on white fails contrast.

Test small

Shrink the logo to 40px. Try it in one color and on dark backgrounds.

Check the name

"Zero Gravity" is common. Search app stores and trademark records before investing.
